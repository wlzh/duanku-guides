```markdown
Dear Schwab Team,
Please find my attached W-8BEN form. I am a resident of China and am submitting this updated form to claim the 10% tax treaty benefit on dividends.
I previously made a mistake in my tax certification, and this version includes my Foreign Tax Identifying Number (FTIN) and the correct treaty claim in Part II. Please update my account's tax status accordingly.
Thank you for your assistance.
Best regards.
```
